# Sandwich Construction

## Assumptions
- We have an unobstructed countertop
- On the counter we have:
    - a plate with 2 slices of bread side-by-side
    - Jar of peanut butter
    - Jar of jelly
    - A butter knife
    - Napkin
    - The robot is charged and ready to go
<br><br>

## Steps to build the sandwich
1. Open the peanut butter
2. Open the jelly
3. Pick up the butter knife
4. Use the butter knife to scoop peanut butter out of the jar
5. Spread peanut butter on the left slice of bread
6. Use the napkin the clean the butter knife
7. Use the butter knife to scoop the jelly out of the jar
8. Spread the jelly on the right slice of bread
9. Use the napkin the clean the butter knife
10. Discard the napkin
11. put the butter knife down
12. Flip the right slice of bread
13. stack the right slice of bread on top of the left slice of bread
14. Replace the lids on the Peanut Butter and jelly
15. Put the Peanut butter, jelly, and knife away
<br><br>

## Exit Conditions
- Plate and Constucted sandwich are the only items on the counter
